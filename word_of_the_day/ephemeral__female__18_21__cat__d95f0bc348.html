ephemeral

ih-FEM-er-uhl · Adjective

Meaning

Lasting for a very short time; fleeting.

Hindi

kshanik

Synonym

transient

Antonym

permanent

In a sentence

  • The fleeting joy of a K-drama cliffhanger is ephemeral, but the plot twists linger.
